Methodology

How the AI engine works

Trained on labeled phishing and benign URL datasets from PhishTank, VirusTotal, and Kaggle.

STEP 01

Feature Extraction

The URL is decomposed into 14+ features: length, HTTPS, special characters, subdomains, IP usage, TLD risk, redirects, and more.

STEP 02

ML Inference

An ensemble of Random Forest, Decision Tree, and XGBoost models scores the feature vector for malicious probability.

STEP 03

Classification

The URL is classified as Safe, Suspicious, Phishing, or Malware — and the specific attack pattern is identified.

STEP 04

Explain & Protect

The system explains why the verdict was reached and recommends targeted prevention and mitigation steps.
